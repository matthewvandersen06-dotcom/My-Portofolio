Game Creation · March 2026

(Binus Game Development) BGDJam 2026

  • Self-initiated
  • Group project
  • Tester and App Advisor

Project description

We were given the theme “Under Pressure” and decided to make a survival game. The player is a fish that shoots its “predators” in order to survive.

Impacts

The game feels exciting and challenging at the same time. The longer people play, the better they get at spotting patterns and surviving. As the levels rise, tension builds and keeps players interested.

Lesson learned

It was really fun, thanks to the positive ecosystem of my groupmates. Testing the game was exciting because it was both interesting and challenging. I also learned to manage my time better, communicate more, take more initiative and become a good listener of other people’s ideas and concepts.

Machine Learning · February – June 2026

Game Recommendation System

  • Work assignment
  • Group project
  • Frontend and Backend Developer

Project description

We built a game recommendation system based on genre, release year, platform and more. It also shows statistics about global and regional game sales.

Impacts

We made this so content creators such as Windah Basudara and Miawaug can easily find games to play based on genre, release year and platform. They can search for the kind of game they need for their content and find it quickly.

Lesson learned

I learned about Cosine Similarity, KNN and K-Means. The system still needs work, such as a larger dataset and more accurate recommendations for user preferences. Even so, I enjoyed this project because it helped me grow a lot in the technical side of artificial intelligence.

Business Case · April – June 2026

AIESEC Future Leaders

  • Self-initiated
  • Group project
  • Business Analyst

Project description

We role-played as business analysts for a noodle supply company facing a reputation problem. Our task was to analyse the causes, our markets and the company’s achievements, then find the best way to overcome the problem. The program included seminars, coaching sessions and presentations.

Impacts

We created a business strategy analysis that can serve as a reference for solving problems in other case studies and support decision-making.

Lesson learned

I learned a lot about social media management, understanding people and negotiating. I became more initiative and communicative, and I focused not only on my tasks but on growing the team through attitude, critical thinking and problem solving. We all grew and gained insights from this project.
